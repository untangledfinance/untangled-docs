# What is Vault on Stellar?
Introduction to Vault on Stellar

**Untangled Vault** is a permissionless, on-chain vault technology on Stellar with **flexibility** and **ease of use** for curators.

## Why Untangled Vault?

On Stellar, there is no vault infrastructure that can combine multiple yield sources such as those from lending, Dex AMM or RWA protocols into a single token that liquidity providers/institutional investors can have exposure to. 

## Quick Look at Vault

-  **Non-custodial** Soroban-based vault technology  
-  **Curators** can independently deploy vaults using the Untangled Curator App  
-  Execute **any strategy** with maximum flexibility  
-  **Deposits** are synchronous, while **withdrawals** are asynchronous  
-  **Role-based management** of the vault: *Issuer, Distributor, Curator*  
-  Integrated with **LOBSTR** and **MPC wallets** (e.g., Fireblocks)  
-  **Open & permissionless** deployment  

Vault enables curators to create **novel yield products** on top of existing protocols to attracts capital allocators, helping increase Stellar’s Total Value Locked (TVL)
