# Welcome to Untangled

Untangled Finance builds credit and yield infrastructure for institutions, on
Stellar and EVM chains, and advises on yield strategies through our in-house
credit investment management, risk modelling and smart contract work. We are
backed by **Fasanara Capital**, an institutional asset manager.

These docs come in two parts. **Products** are what clients use and pay for.
**Protocols** are the contracts and services the products run on.
