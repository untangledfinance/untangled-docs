# Products

Products are what we offer institutions. Each runs on one or more of
Untangled's [protocols](/docs/protocols).

<div className={styles.cardGrid}>

<Link className={styles.card} to="/docs/Prime/welcome-to-prime">
  <h3>Untangled Prime</h3>
  <p>A smart account for institutions and their agents. Custody keeps the funds; the account checks each agent's rules on chain. Live on Stellar mainnet, with an EVM version on testnet.</p>
</Link>

<Link className={styles.card} to="/docs/payfi">
  <h3>Prime PayFi</h3>
  <p>Lends a payment orchestrator 90% of each payout while the sender's fiat clears, through a Prime account and an OctoLend market. Public beta on Stellar testnet.</p>
</Link>

</div>

| Product | Status | Runs on |
|---|---|---|
| [Untangled Prime](/docs/Prime/welcome-to-prime) | Stellar mainnet and testnet; EVM on Base Sepolia testnet | [OctoGate](/docs/OctoGate/prime-contracts), OpenZeppelin smart accounts |
| [Prime PayFi](/docs/payfi) | Public beta on Stellar testnet; Stellar mainnet and other networks planned | [OctoLend](/docs/OctoLend/lending-market), [OctoGate](/docs/OctoGate/prime-contracts), Prime accounts |
