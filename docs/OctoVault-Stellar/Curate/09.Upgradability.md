# Upgradability 
OctoVaults are non-upgradable. 

## Migrate a Vault
The vault is state-less. However, you can migrate by
- Removing share allowance for the vault from Distributor Account 
- The current share can be migrated to a new vault


## Q&A
Can we make an OctoVault immutable?
Yes, OctoVault is immutable.

How can we keep track of new OctoVault version releases?
You can track new smart contract releases on our github.
