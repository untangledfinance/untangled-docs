# What is Untangled OctoVault on Stellar?

**Untangled Vault** is a permissionless, on-chain vault technology focused on **flexibility** and **ease of use** for curators.

---

## Why Untangled OctoVault?

On Stellar, there is currently **no vault infrastructure** that can combine multiple yield sources—such as those from **lending**, **DEX AMMs**, or **RWA protocols**—into a single token.  
OctoVault solves this by enabling liquidity providers and institutional investors to gain **exposure to diversified yield strategies** through one vault.

---

## Quick Look at Stellar OctoVault

-  **Non-custodial** Soroban-based vault technology  
-  **Curators** can independently deploy vaults using the [Untangled Curator App](#)  
-  Execute **any strategy** with maximum flexibility  
-  **Deposits** are synchronous, while **withdrawals** are asynchronous  
-  **Role-based management** of the vault: *Issuer, Distributor, Curator*  
-  Integrated with **LOBSTR** and **MPC wallets** (e.g., Fireblocks)  
-  Enables curators to create **novel yield products** on top of existing protocols  
-  Attracts capital allocators, helping increase **Stellar’s Total Value Locked (TVL)**  
-  **Open & permissionless** deployment
