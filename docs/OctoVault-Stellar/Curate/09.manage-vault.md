# Manage Your Vault

Go to **curator-stellar@untangled.finance**

This interface allows you to:

- **Create a new vault**  
- **View vault performance**  
- **View integrated protocols (markets) and make allocations**  
  - Allocations to the following protocols are automatically picked up in the valuation:  
    - OctoLend (coming soon)  
    - Blend (Fixed Pool V2, YieldBlox Pool V2)  
    - Aquarius  
    - DeFi Hub  
    - SoroSwap  
    - StellarX  
    - Stellar Term  
  - 👉 If you need more integrations, reach out to our team  

- **Update share price**  
  - Untangled back-end calculates and updates the price of **USDyc**  
  - ⚠️ This currently requires custody of the distributor wallet’s private key  
  - Future iterations will:  
    - Enable curators to view price before using a **multisig for approval (manual)**  
    - Provide a service for Curators to **run their own price update** on their own systems  

## Pause a Vault

- The vault is **not pausable** because it is **stateless and immutable**  
- However, you can **migrate the existing share to a new vault**
