# Introducing OctoLend

## Abstract
- OctoLend is a curated lending protocol on Stellar/Soroban.
- Collateral Providers stake XLM (later RWAs) in a Collateral Vault, receiving sXLM.
- Borrowers post their own collateral and can tap additional collateral automatically from the vault.
- Lenders (LPs) supply USDC to Lending Markets and receive Market Shares.
- Market has an customisable interest rate model (based on utilisation) which can also be fixed rate

Risk is managed by on-chain LTV/HF checks:
- Borrower: repay anytime, free up collateral.
- Market liquidate if HF < 1 or LTV ≥ LLTV
Losses are absorbed first by Borrower’s own collateral, then by the Collateral Vault. Market LPs only suffer if all locked collateral is exhausted.

## Collateral Vault
- Providers stake XLM (later RWAs) → mint sXLM.
### States of XLM:
- Free: transferable, withdrawable anytime.
- Delegated: when XLM is delegated to a Borrower
- Allocated: backing an active DT. Released when the loan is repaid.

### Withdrawals: 
Any Free sXLM can be redeemed for XLM.

### Parameters: 
Borrower-specific:
- Delegation fee (time-accrued, paid in USDC).
- Minimum borrower self-collateral ratio.
- Delegation multiple (how much collateral from the vault can back each borrower).

## Lending Market
A vault pooling USDC from LPs. Peer-to-pool: multiple LPs, multiple borrowers. Defined by:
- Collateral token: sXLM.
- Debt denomination: USDC.
- Interest rate model (which can be configured as fixed rate borrow)

### NAV-based accounting: 
LP deposits mint Market Shares; withdrawals are subject to available liquidity.

### Debt 
- Not tokenized but a book keeping entry

## Loan Lifecycle
### Borrow
- Borrower selects the market, posts own collateral and specifies the amount to borrow.
- If a collateral shortfall exists, the borrower can use delegated XLM (Delegated → Allocated).
- Market transfers USDC from its pool to the borrower; record debt.

### Repay
- Borrower sends USDC, DT is burned, Allocated XLM becomes Delegated, borrower’s collateral returned.

## Liquidation
If HF < 1 or LTV ≥ LLTV: anyone can liquidate:
- Seize borrower’s collateral. Transfer needed XLM from vault (burn Allocated sXLM).
- Repay borrow by sending USDC. Burn DT.
- Socialize any residual loss across Collateral Vault (Market only loses if all locked collateral depleted).

## Collateral Vault Mechanics
XLM always stays in the Vault unless:
- Withdrawn by a provider against Free sXLM, or
- Seized in liquidation (sent to liquidator in exchange for USDC repayment to the market)

## Risk & Governance
- Borrower-specific risk controls: Borrower’s rating determines debt ceilings, delegation multiples (cap on vault-backed collateral per borrower), delegation fees → higher fees for riskier borrowers.
- Liquidation safety net: Borrower collateral is seized first; vault collateral second.
- Loss absorption waterfall: Borrower → Collateral Vault → Market LPs (only if vault is exhausted).

## User Experience
### Collateral Providers
- Stake XLM → sXLM.
- Passive: Earn delegation fees.
- Withdraw Free sXLM anytime.

### Borrowers
- Post collateral, borrow USDC instantly.
- Repay anytime 
- Maintain HF above threshold to avoid liquidation.

### Lenders (LPs)
- Deposit USDC → Market Shares.
- Passive: earn interest accretion 
- Withdraw pro-rata; async if liquidity is tied in loans.

## Roadmap
- v1: Single Collateral, pooled Collateral Vault, HF-based liquidation, async withdrawals with epochs, variable rate loan IRM, borrower-level parameters.
- v2: Multi-collateral (RWA with PoR).

## Summary
OctoLend enables curated lending market on Stellar with:
- Pooled liquidity from LPs.
- Delegated collateral support via XLM.
- Customisable Interest Rate Model for different use cases
- Clear loss waterfall: borrower first, then collateral vault, then LPs.
