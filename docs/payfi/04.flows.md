# Flows

F is a pay-in's face value, B the loan (90% of F) and T the PO's top-up (10% of
F). The code for each flow is in [Flows in code](./06.integration/04.flows-in-code.md).

## A payout, in two transactions

The custody gate pays only the addresses fixed in its list when it was
deployed, and the execution contract refuses any batch that names another
address. A receiver chosen at payout time cannot appear in that batch, so the
payout is split. The **advance** runs through the execution contract and names
no receiver. The **release** is a plain stablecoin transfer from the settlement
account.

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant PO as PO backend
  participant SV as Signing service
  participant CH as Stellar
  participant RC as Receiver
  PO->>SV: prepare: reference, face F, corridor,<br/>receiver, expected settlement
  Note over SV: screen the receiver, check the limits,<br/>build the advance: B = 90% of F, T = 10% of F
  SV-->>PO: advance step (nothing recorded yet)
  Note over PO: SDK checks the step against the PO's figures,<br/>the operations key signs it
  PO->>SV: signed step
  Note over SV: rebuild the batch, accept only an exact match,<br/>screen again, check the live limits,<br/>co-sign the advance and the mint
  SV->>CH: submit the advance, pay the fee
  Note over CH: rules check cover, price age, line cap.<br/>Mint F POT, post it, borrow B,<br/>pull T from custody
  CH-->>SV: settlement account holds F
  SV-->>PO: funded, release ready
  PO->>SV: ask for the release step
  SV-->>PO: release step: F to this receiver
  Note over PO: SDK checks it, the operations key<br/>signs the whole transaction
  PO->>CH: release: settlement account pays F
  CH->>RC: F
  SV->>CH: read the release
  SV-->>PO: paid
```

Nothing is recorded when a payout is prepared, and the step must be signed
within about a minute (12 ledgers) or prepared again. The pay-in exists once the
PO's operations key has signed the advance step. A release stays valid for 60
ledgers, about five minutes; once it lapses the PO asks for a renewal for the
same receiver and amount. Several payouts can be in flight at once, up to
15,000 funded and unreleased in total, because one release moves at most that
much.

## Repayment by the PO alone

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant FI as Fiat clears
  participant CU as PO custody account
  participant PO as PO backend
  participant CH as Stellar
  participant MK as Lending market
  participant SV as Signing service
  FI->>CU: F
  PO->>CH: repayment batch, signed by the<br/>operations key alone
  CH->>CU: the gate pulls at most the POT withdrawn
  CH->>MK: repay the debt
  CH->>MK: withdraw F POT
  Note over CH: burn F POT
  SV->>CH: read the repayment
  SV-->>PO: advances closed
```

The PO repays with its own key, and the signing service plays no part on chain,
so a fault on Untangled's side never blocks a repayment. One repayment can close
several advances. A partial repayment must close at least one advance whole;
[Flows in code](./06.integration/04.flows-in-code.md#repay-part) gives the
exact limit. A repayment closes only advances whose money has left the
settlement account, so release an advance before repaying it.

## Overdue suspension

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant PO as PO backend
  participant SV as Signing service
  participant CH as Stellar
  Note over PO,CH: advance O-1 is open past its expected settlement
  PO->>SV: payout for pay-in O-2
  SV-->>PO: refused: O-1 is overdue, nothing signed
  PO->>CH: repay O-1, PO alone
  SV-->>PO: O-1 closed
  PO->>SV: payout for O-2 again
  SV->>CH: co-sign and submit
```

An advance open past its expected settlement suspends every new payout until it
is repaid. The same suspension applies while the market's loan-to-value is
above 92%, and while the settlement account holds funds outside the expected
flow, until both owners sweep them back to custody.

## Withdrawing cover

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant PO as PO
  participant SV as Signing service
  participant CH as Stellar
  PO->>SV: withdraw: asset, amount
  Note over SV: cover after the withdrawal<br/>at least 20% of the debt?
  SV-->>PO: withdrawal step
  PO->>SV: signed step
  SV->>CH: the asset to custody, through the gated execution contract
  Note over CH: the withdraw rule checks the 20% again
```

## Liquidation

When the cover is below 15% of the debt, anyone can liquidate, alone and in one
transaction. The liquidator gets a quote, builds the five-call batch, signs it
once as the transaction's source and sends it. The SDK does all of it from chain
reads, and the signing service offers the same transaction as a step that it
only relays. The quote leaves a buffer of 50 basis points under the rule's 105%
edge, so a small price rise before the transaction runs still passes.

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant LQ as Liquidator
  participant SDK as SDK or public API
  participant GF as Gate-free execution contract
  participant ST as Settlement account and its rules
  participant MK as Market and tokens
  participant SV as Signing service
  LQ->>SDK: quote: debt, prices and basket read now
  SDK-->>LQ: repay R up to half the debt,<br/>receive y under 105% of R
  LQ->>SDK: build the five calls
  SDK-->>LQ: unsigned transaction, simulated
  LQ->>GF: one signed transaction:<br/>R in, asset out, repay, withdraw, burn
  GF->>ST: asks approval
  Note over ST: liquidate rule: cover below 15%,<br/>R at most half the debt,<br/>y at most 105% of R, prices fresh
  ST->>MK: repay R, withdraw and burn R POT
  MK-->>LQ: the asset
  Note over SV: reads the liquidation from the chain,<br/>records it once, credits R to the open advances
```

The signing service learns of each liquidation from the chain and records it
once, whoever sent it.

## Take-over after a default

If the PO defaults, Untangled queues a take-over, waits, and then runs it. The
chain judges the terms when it runs.

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant SV as Signing service and Untangled admin
  participant GA as Gated execution contract
  participant PO as PO
  participant RC as Recovery account
  participant ST as Settlement account and its rules
  SV->>GA: queue the take: repay R, take an asset,<br/>both sign
  Note over GA,PO: the wait: 120 ledgers on testnet.<br/>The PO can repay, and cancels<br/>the take-over once its debt is 0
  RC->>GA: run, as the transaction's source
  GA->>ST: asks approval
  Note over ST: at run time: R at most the debt,<br/>price fresh and in band,<br/>asset at most 105% of R
  ST-->>RC: the asset, the debt falls by R
```

A repayment during the wait, or a price that moved, makes the run fail whole.
The 120-ledger wait (about ten minutes) is a testnet setting; a production line
sets its own.

## Freeze

Untangled's admin can freeze a line with a reason and lift the freeze later.
While a line is frozen the service signs no advance, release or withdrawal.
Repayment, liquidation and take-over keep working, so a freeze never traps the
PO's debt or its cover.
