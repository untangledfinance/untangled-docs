# Prime PayFi

Prime PayFi lends a payment orchestrator (PO) 90% of each payout while the
sender's fiat clears. Liquidity providers fund the 90% through a lending market
open only to that PO. The PO adds the other 10% from its own custody account,
pays the receiver in full at once, and repays the loan when the fiat arrives one
to three days later.

Every advance, release and repayment is a Stellar transaction. The PO's
borrowing account is a Prime smart account, and its on-chain rules check each
payout before it runs: the loan size, the PO's collateral cover, the price feeds
and the line's cap. Untangled's signing service screens each receiver and ties
each advance to the pay-in it funds.

## Status

Prime PayFi is in public beta on **Stellar testnet**, with a test dollar (TUSD)
in place of a real stablecoin. Stellar mainnet and other networks are planned.
The SDK and the signing service refuse mainnet today, and the beta's figures
are testnet settings that a production line will set again. The items left
before real money are listed in
[Limits and protections](./05.limits-and-protections.md#before-real-money).

## Built on Untangled protocols

Prime PayFi uses existing Untangled contracts, configured for one PO per line.

- **[OctoLend](../OctoLend/03.lending-market.md):** the lending market that
  holds the liquidity providers' money and lends against the PO's payment
  obligations.
- **[Untangled Prime](../Prime/02.how-prime-works.md):** the smart account that
  borrows, with its policy rules, execution contract and custody gate. The
  contract mechanics are on the OctoGate page
  [Prime contracts on Stellar](../OctoGate/prime-contracts.md).

The beta adds one small contract of its own: an admin-gated price feed.
