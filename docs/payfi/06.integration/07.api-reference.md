## Conventions

- Amounts are decimal strings of whole tokens with at most 7 decimals: "1000",
  "0.5". Ledger numbers and seconds are integers.
- Every answer carries `request_id`, also sent as the `X-Request-Id` header.
- Lists are cursor-paged: `?limit=` (50 by default, at most 200) and `?cursor=`
  from the previous page's `next_cursor`, which is null on the last page.
- The service answers under `/v1/` at the service's root. The app's `/api/v1/`
  serves the same routes with a tighter limit on changes.
- Each call that changes state carries an `Idempotency-Key`
  ([Idempotency](./05.steps-and-terms.md#idempotency)).
