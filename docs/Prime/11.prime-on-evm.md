# Prime on EVM

Prime also runs on EVM chains, in its own app at
[beta-evm.untangled.finance](https://beta-evm.untangled.finance). The idea is the same as
on Stellar: your own account, rules you set, and funds that stay in your custody. The
parts underneath are different, so several steps work differently. This page covers
the EVM app, and the table at the end sets the two side by side.

:::caution
Prime on EVM runs on the Base Sepolia test network only. There is no EVM mainnet
deployment. Use test funds.
:::

## What a Prime Account is on EVM

On EVM a Prime Account is a Safe smart account with an execution contract attached: a
Zodiac Roles module that every agent move goes through, and that refuses anything
outside the permission the move runs under.

The account holds no funds and no allowance. Money moves only from your custody gate
to a venue and back, and a lending position belongs to custody, not to the Prime
Account.

## Connect a wallet

Select **Connect a wallet** and choose MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, or Trust
Wallet, or use WalletConnect to pair a wallet on your phone. An **Agent wallet** option
appears as well once an agent key has been placed in the browser. Your wallet must be
on Base Sepolia; the app will not sign on any other network.

## Create a Prime Account

Open **Accounts** and select **Create a Prime Account**. The wizard has the same steps
as on Stellar: Account, Add signers, Approvals, and Review. Finishing it takes one
transaction, paid in ETH by the wallet you connected, which becomes the first signer.
That transaction creates the account and its execution contract together.

![The Accounts page with a Prime Account open, showing Manage allowance and Manage execution](/img/prime/evm-accounts.png)

Open the account under **Accounts** to reach two dialogs:

- **Manage execution** shows the execution contract and whether it is live. **Switch
  off** stops every agent at once. Switching it off or back on is a change to the
  account, so it needs the account's approvals.
- **Manage allowance** only checks and revokes. The account is never granted an
  allowance, and if an old one is found, **Revoke** sets it to zero.

![The Manage execution dialog, showing the execution contract live on Base Sepolia](/img/prime/evm-manage-execution.png)

## Signers and approvals

Signers are managed from the **Signers** tab, as on Stellar. On EVM you change one
signer at a time - add or remove one, and set the approval count - and each change is a
single transaction. On an account that needs more than one approval, the change waits
in **Transactions - Queue** for the other signers.

## Policies

On EVM the only policy you can add is the **Custody-funded action**. The other cards
stay off, and the app says why: **Spending limit**, the Aave supply and withdraw cards,
and **Add a custom policy** would spend the account's own balance, and a Prime Account
on EVM holds none.

![The Policies tab on EVM, with the Custody-funded action available and the other templates off](/img/prime/evm-policies.png)

## Set up a custody gate

On EVM the custody gate is a Safe of its own. It holds the funds under three keys: a
primary key, a break-glass key, and a recovery key. Any two of the three approve, and
one key alone moves nothing. Prime is not an owner.

With no custody gate yet, **Policies** → **Custody-funded action** opens a four-step
set-up:

- **Custody:** the three keys. The wallet you connected is the primary key. It can
  create the gate, but it cannot move anything out of it.
- **Destinations:** the Prime Account the gate serves, and the agent who acts through
  it. The venues are Aave v3 and Uniswap v3 (USDC to WETH), and everything comes back
  to custody.
- **Limit:** a budget in USDC that renews every set number of days, up to 3,650, and
  the most a single move may be. The budget renews; it does not expire.
- **Review:** everything above, shown before anything is signed.

**Create gate** does it all in one transaction, paid by the connected wallet: it
creates the custody gate and gives the agent its first permission, for lending up to
the per-move limit. If your Prime Account needs more than one approval, its side of
that permission waits in **Transactions** for the other signers. The gate holds test
USDC only.

![The custody view: the custody Safe, its three keys at 2 of 3, its balance, and the mandate](/img/prime/evm-custody-view.png)

## Give an agent a permission

Once the gate exists, **Policies** → **Custody-funded action** opens **Let an agent
move custody's money**. Each permission covers one band:

- **What it may do:** Aave supply and withdraw, or a Uniswap swap from USDC to WETH.
- **Amount band:** from the first amount up to, but not including, the second, for
  every single move.
- **Who may act:** an agent key, which acts alone in this band, or a shared account
  whose owners must all approve each move - for example the agent and an admin, 2 of
  2. You make a shared account with **Create a Prime Account**.

A permission names one address, so a band that needs two people names a shared
account, not two keys. Installing a permission is a change to the Prime Account, so on an
account that needs more than one approval it waits for the other signers.

![The Let an agent move custody's money dialog, with a swap band from 0 to 100 USDC](/img/prime/evm-band-dialog.png)

## Run a move with Prime Execution

**Apps** → **Prime Execution** runs up to two calls as one transaction through the
account's execution contract.

1. Under **Authorised by**, choose the permission to act under.
2. Edit **Call 1**: supply, withdraw, or swap, and the amount. For a swap, **Use live
   quote** sets the least you will accept to 1% under the current quote.
3. Press **Execute**. The app checks the call against the permission first. A move over
   the band's limit is refused here, with nothing signed or sent.
4. Read **Review before you sign**, then press **Run**. For a band held by a shared
   account the button is **Sign and queue**, and the other owners approve from a
   co-sign link.

An agent that is not a signer of the Prime Account adds it once, from the **Prime
Account** button, with **Connect Safe smart account**.

![Prime Execution, with a swap permission chosen under Authorised by](/img/prime/evm-prime-execution.png)

## Transactions and co-signing

**Transactions** has **Queue** and **History**, as on Stellar. Opening a queued item
shows **It runs, in order**, which spells each call out in words - for example, custody
approves the Aave pool for 150 USDC, then custody supplies 150 USDC to Aave. Select
**Approve** to sign. The last approver submits it with **Execute Safe transaction**.

## Connected apps

You can pair an app with the Prime Account over WalletConnect. On EVM an app may only
ask the account to sign a message, such as a sign-in or an off-chain order, and the
owners approve that signature. A request to send a transaction as the account is shown
to you and then declined.

**Apps** → **Safe sign-in** has the owners sign one message and the Safe verify it.
Nothing moves, and no session with an app is opened.

## Get funds back without Prime

The custody view has **Recover funds**. With two of the three custody keys you can move
funds out of custody, or withdraw the whole Aave position back to it, without the Prime
Account or the agent. **Test one-key refusal** shows that one key on its own moves
nothing. Once the gate exists, open the custody view from
[the custody gate page](https://beta-evm.untangled.finance/#/onboard/evm/gate) with
**Open custody view**.

## Where EVM differs from Stellar

| | Stellar | EVM |
|---|---|---|
| Network | Stellar | Base Sepolia testnet only |
| The account | A Prime Account contract | A Safe with an execution contract |
| Custody | Your custody wallet, and a gate you deploy that it grants limits to | A custody Safe held by three keys, 2 of 3, created with the agent's first permission in one transaction |
| Budget | An allowance per asset, for a set number of days | One USDC budget that renews every set number of days |
| Venues | Blend and Aquarius | Aave v3, and Uniswap v3 for USDC to WETH |
| Where a lending position sits | With the Prime Account | With custody |
| Policy templates | Several, and custom policies | The Custody-funded action only |
| Two people for large moves | The band sets how many approvals it needs | The band names a shared account whose owners all approve |
| Swap minimum | Fixed when the permission is written | Set per swap from the live quote |
| Batch size | A sequence of calls | Up to two calls |
| Changing signers | Two transactions | One signer per change, in one transaction |
| Connected apps | Requests are checked against the account's rules | Signing only; transaction requests are declined |
