# Accounts and portfolio

## Accounts

The **Accounts** page lists your connected wallet and every Prime Account it
owns. Select **Create a Prime Account** to set up a new one.

![The Accounts page listing a wallet and a Prime Account](/img/prime/accounts-page.png)

Open a Prime Account to see its detail: the account address, its owner, and
shortcuts to its policies and transactions. **Manage allowance** is here too - it
controls how much a template such as Blend supply or Pull funds may draw from
your connected wallet.

![An expanded Prime Account, showing its address, owner, and shortcuts](/img/prime/account-detail.png)

## Where your funds live

Your funds stay in your custody wallet - the wallet you connected. A Prime
Account does not hold them. Instead, you grant the account an allowance on your
custody wallet with **Manage allowance**, and its policies act within that
allowance: a template such as Blend supply or Pull funds draws from the custody
wallet through the execution adapter, and the resulting position belongs to the
Prime Account. See [Apps and execution](./07.apps-and-execution.md).

## Portfolio

The **Portfolio** page watches balances across addresses you add - up to twenty
EVM, Stellar, or NEAR addresses. The addresses stay in your browser, and only the
address is sent to the portfolio service to look up balances.

![The Portfolio page before any address is added](/img/prime/portfolio.png)
