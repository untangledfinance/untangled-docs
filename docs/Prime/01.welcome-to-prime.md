# Welcome to Prime

Treasuries, funds and fintechs that keep assets with an MPC custodian such as
Fordefi or Fireblocks, or in their own multisig, have had three ways to put
those assets to work in DeFi. Each one gives something up:

| Option | What it costs |
|---|---|
| Move the funds to a hot wallet | It is fast, but the custody controls and the approval chain stay behind. |
| Sign every move with the full quorum | It is safe, but slow. No operator or agent can act, so positions go unmanaged. |
| Trust a vendor's off-chain policy | The limits live in a vendor dashboard, where the chain and the venue cannot see or enforce them. |

Prime keeps your custody arrangement and makes execution programmable. The
funds stay in your custody wallet. A Prime Account, a multisig smart account on
Stellar, may use them only within rules that the chain enforces and that anyone
can read.

## What you can do with it

| You want to | Prime gives you |
|---|---|
| Keep funds in your MPC wallet or multisig | A custody gate. Custody approves a limit, an expiry and a list of destinations once, and the gate releases nothing outside them. |
| Let an operator or an agent act without full keys | Rules that name the venue, the asset, an amount band and who must approve. |
| Give large moves a second look | A time lock. A move is stored on chain before it runs, and custody can cancel it. |
| Keep the funds reachable if custody's signers lose access | Recovery to one address, fixed when the gate is created. |
| Pull, swap and pay back in one step | An execution contract that runs the whole batch as one transaction, checked before anything moves. |

## Who it is for

| Who | What they get |
|---|---|
| Treasuries and funds | Lending, borrowing and swaps on chain while the funds stay in custody. |
| Venues | Access to custodied liquidity, with each account's limits readable on chain. |
| Operators and agents | Room to manage positions inside amount bands and the time lock, without holding full keys. |

## Where it stands

| Area | Today |
|---|---|
| App | The beta app at [beta.untangled.finance](https://beta.untangled.finance), on Stellar testnet |
| Accounts | OpenZeppelin smart accounts on Stellar. [Prime on EVM](./06.prime-on-evm.md) uses Safe, on Base Sepolia only. |
| Policy layer | OctoGate, our own, built on OpenZeppelin account policies |
| Custody | Your own multisig, or an MPC wallet such as Fordefi or Fireblocks |
| Venues | Aquarius and Blend on Stellar |
| Security | Internal review and a threat model are done. The contracts have not had an external audit yet. |

:::caution
The flows in these docs run on Stellar testnet in the beta app. Use test funds,
and read [Security](./05.security.md) before you rely on Prime with real value.
:::

## Watch it first

We recorded seven short videos on the beta app on 1 October 2026, each under two
minutes. Together they take one Prime Account from nothing to an agent working
inside its rules. Each walkthrough page carries its video.

| # | Walkthrough | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Create a Prime Account](./03.walkthrough/01.create-a-prime-account.md) | Three signers, two approvals, one transaction |
| 2 | [Set up the custody gate](./03.walkthrough/02.set-up-the-custody-gate.md) | Custody approves a limit, a time lock, a venue and a recovery address |
| 3 | [Create the execution contract](./03.walkthrough/03.create-the-execution-contract.md) | The signers create the contract that runs batches through the gate |
| 4 | [Install rules for an agent](./03.walkthrough/04.install-rules-for-an-agent.md) | Three swap rules, sized by amount |
| 5 | [Run a move](./03.walkthrough/05.run-a-move.md) | A small swap runs, a swap too large for its rule is refused, a large swap runs once the Admin co-signs |
| 6 | [Use the time lock](./03.walkthrough/06.use-the-time-lock.md) | Two swaps are stored, custody cancels one, the other runs |
| 7 | [Recover custody funds](./03.walkthrough/07.recover-custody-funds.md) | The Prime signers move funds to the recovery address without custody's keys |
