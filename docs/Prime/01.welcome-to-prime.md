# Welcome to Prime

Prime is for institutions that hold assets with an MPC custodian such as Fordefi
or Fireblocks, or in their own multisig, and want to lend or swap on chain
without moving those assets to a hot wallet or having every signer approve
every trade.

You create a Prime Account, a multisig smart account on Stellar.
Custody then sets up a custody gate that lets the account draw on custody's
funds within limits custody chooses. The account's signers add rules for what
an agent or operator may do, and the account contract checks those rules on
chain before anything runs.

## What a setup includes

Custody sets up the gate once, with the venues funds may go to, a time lock and,
optionally, a recovery address. These can't be changed later. Custody also
grants the gate an allowance per asset, which it can change or revoke whenever
it likes.

A rule sets a venue, an amount band, who approves and an optional wait. Moves
that have to wait are stored on chain, and custody or the account's signers can
cancel them before they run.

Moves run through the account's execution contract, which can pull from
custody, swap and send the proceeds back in one transaction.

## Current status

Prime runs on Stellar mainnet at
[app.untangled.finance](https://app.untangled.finance), with Aquarius and Blend
pools as venues. The same app runs on Stellar testnet at
[beta.untangled.finance](https://beta.untangled.finance), where you can try a
setup with test funds first.

Prime's contracts (OctoGate) have been through our internal adversarial review
and threat modelling, and an external audit is in progress. An EVM version runs on the Base Sepolia testnet;
see [Prime on EVM](./06.prime-on-evm.md).
