# Transactions and co-signing

Every move on a Prime Account is a transaction the account approves against its
rules. **Transactions** has two tabs: **Queue**, for what is waiting, and
**History**, for what has already happened. The Queue shows items for the Prime
Account picked with the **Prime Account** button at the top of the page.

## When one signature is enough

If the connected wallet alone meets the approval count, the app builds the
transaction, your wallet signs it, and the app sends it. It shows each step as it
happens: build, simulate, sign, broadcast and confirm.

## What waits in the Queue

| Item | Why it waits | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A co-sign request | The Prime Account needs more approvals than the wallet that started it could give | Each other signer opens its co-sign link and signs. |
| A custody transaction | Custody's account needs more signing weight than the key that started it carries, as in the gate set-up or a cancel | Each of custody's other signers selects **Sign with active wallet**; the last selects **Submit**. |
| A delayed move | A move stored under a time lock is waiting to run | Once it is ready, anyone can select **Run**. Until then, custody or the account's signers can cancel it. See [Use the time lock](../03.walkthrough/06.use-the-time-lock.md). |

## Co-sign links

When a transaction needs more approvals than your wallet can give, the app does
not send it to fail on chain. It attaches your signature and shows **More
signatures needed**: who has signed, who can still sign, and a co-sign link that
expires in about 24 hours.

![More signatures needed, with the co-sign link](/img/prime/more-signatures-needed.webp)

1. Send the link to the other signers however you like. The request is also
   saved in the Queue, so anyone can copy the link from there later.
2. Each signer opens the link, checks the transaction and selects **Sign and
   continue**.
3. Once the approval count is met, the transaction is sent.

## What a co-signer sees

The co-sign page decodes the transaction itself and reads the account fresh from
the chain. It does not trust anything the link says about itself, so a link that
arrives in a message cannot describe one call and carry another. The page shows
the function, its arguments exactly as signed, the account it acts as, the rule,
and every approver's address in full, so you can check each one.

![The co-sign page](/img/prime/cosign-review.webp)

For a batch, the page lists each call in order, so you approve the whole batch
having read every call in it.

## Custody's signatures

When custody is a multisig, its transactions show the weight collected so far
and which of custody's signers can still sign. Each one connects, opens the item
and selects **Sign with active wallet**. When the weight is met, the item reads
ready, and the last signer selects **Submit**.

![A custody transaction waiting for weight in the Queue](/img/prime/queue-custody-weight.webp)

## Two signers at the same time

If two signers open the same co-sign link at once, the app holds a short lock so
their signatures do not collide. A signer who arrives while another is signing
is asked to wait a moment.
