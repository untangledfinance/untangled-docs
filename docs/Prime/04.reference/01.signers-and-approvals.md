# Signers and approvals

Signers are the wallets allowed to act on your Prime Account. The approval count
is how many of them must agree before the account does anything. Both are on the
**Signers** tab under **Policies**.

## Read the current set-up

The Signers tab lists every signer and the approval count in force. A line such
as "a change needs 2 of 3 approvals" means the account has three signers and any
two of them together can approve a change.

Each signer shows how many rules it sits on. A signer of the account itself sits
on several; an agent key sits only on the rule that limits it.

![The Signers tab for a 2 of 3 account](/img/prime/signers-tab.png)

## Change signers or the approval count

Select **Manage signers** to add a signer, remove one, or change the approval
count. The dialog shows the current signers and approval count, and sums up the
change before you apply it.

![The Manage signers dialog](/img/prime/manage-signers.png)

A change takes two transactions, and the dialog shows both. The chain cannot
edit the account's signer rule in place, so the app installs the new set of
signers first and then retires the old one. The second step needs the new set's
approval, which may need different people from the first, so the app does not
hide the two steps behind one button. If a step fails partway, the dialog shows
which one landed, and you can carry on from there.

Each step is a transaction the account must approve. If your wallet cannot meet
the approval count alone, the app gives you a co-sign link for the other signers
instead of sending a transaction the chain would refuse. See
[Transactions and co-signing](./03.transactions-and-co-signing.md).

We checked this on Stellar testnet with a 2 of 2 account: one signature was
refused, two were accepted, and a wallet that was not a signer was refused.

## After you change signers

Changing the signers on a rule that carries a policy stops that policy working
until you install the rule again. The policy refuses rather than apply to a
different set of people from the one you chose. See
[Policies](./02.policies.md).
