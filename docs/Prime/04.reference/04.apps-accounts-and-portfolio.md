# Apps, accounts and portfolio

## Apps

**Apps** is where your Prime Account reaches a venue, such as a lending pool or a
swap pool. You browse venues by network. **Prime Execution**, covered in
[Run a move](../03.walkthrough/05.run-a-move.md), is on this page too.

![The Apps page](/img/prime/apps-page.png)

### Connected apps

You can connect an outside app to your Prime Account over WalletConnect, the way
you would connect an ordinary wallet. The app then sees the Prime Account as the
wallet it is acting for, and each request it makes comes back to Prime, where the
account's rules apply before anything is signed.

Open **Connected apps** from the Apps toolbar and paste the connection link from
the app you want to use.

![The Connected apps dialog](/img/prime/connect-apps.png)

However a request reaches the account, it is still a transaction you approve.
The app shows what the call does before you sign, and if the account needs more
approvals, it goes through [co-signing](./03.transactions-and-co-signing.md).

## Accounts

**Accounts** lists your connected wallet and every Prime Account it belongs to.
**Create a Prime Account** starts a new one. Expand a Prime Account to see its
address and reach two dialogs:

| Dialog | What it does |
|---|---|
| Manage execution | Shows the execution contract and the custody gate it works through, and the gate's wait and run window. Creates the execution contract, lets it run stored moves, and connects a gate set up in another browser. See [Create the execution contract](../03.walkthrough/03.create-the-execution-contract.md). |
| Manage allowance | Sets how much a template such as Blend supply or Pull funds may draw from your connected wallet. Custody-funded actions do not use it: they draw through the custody gate. |

![Manage execution with the contract live](/img/prime/manage-execution.webp)

## Stellar Wallet

Stellar Wallet is a wallet built into the app, meant for an agent that drives the
app in a browser. **Connect a wallet** offers it once a key exists in this
browser, and always on testnet, where it can set one up. Each request to sign
opens an approval card showing what is being signed, with **View details** for
the full request.

:::caution
The key lives in the page. Any script running on the page can ask it to sign
while the page is open, so the approval card alone cannot protect it. Keep only
what you are willing to lose on a Stellar Wallet key, and give it power through
a Prime rule with a small amount band rather than through its balance.
:::

While Stellar Wallet is connected, a Stellar button in the page header opens its
panel:

| In the panel | What it does |
|---|---|
| Balances | The wallet's address, network and balances |
| Manage assets | Add or remove a trustline, such as USDC. A trustline can only be removed once its balance is zero. |
| Manage allowance | Opens the Manage allowance dialog for the Prime Account in use |
| Wallets | Add another key and switch between keys. Adding a key works on testnet only. |

A notice in the top right of the page says when the wallet connects, disconnects
or switches to another key.

## Portfolio

**Portfolio** watches balances across up to twenty EVM, Stellar or NEAR
addresses you add. The list stays in your browser; only the addresses are sent
to the portfolio service to look up balances.

![The Portfolio page before any address is added](/img/prime/portfolio.png)
