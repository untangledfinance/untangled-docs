# Apps, accounts and portfolio

## Apps

The **Apps** page lists venues by network, such as lending pools and swap pools.
**Prime Execution** is also here; see [Run a move](../03.walkthrough/05.run-a-move.md).

![The Apps page](/img/prime/apps-page.png)

:::note
Pairing an app is temporarily unavailable, and the **Connected apps** button is
hidden. This section describes how pairing works when it returns.
:::

You can connect an outside app to the Prime Account with WalletConnect. Open
**Connected apps** from the Apps toolbar and paste the app's connection link. The
app sees the Prime Account as its wallet, and you approve its requests like any
other transaction.

![The Connected apps dialog](/img/prime/connect-apps.png)

## Accounts

The **Accounts** page lists your wallet and the Prime Accounts it is a signer of.
**Create a Prime Account** starts a new one. Each account has two buttons when
you expand it.

**Manage execution** shows the execution contract and the custody gate it uses,
with the gate's wait and run window. You use it to create the execution contract,
to let it run stored moves, and to connect a gate that was set up in another
browser. See [Create the execution contract](../03.walkthrough/03.create-the-execution-contract.md).

**Manage allowance** sets how much templates such as Pull funds can take from
your connected wallet. Templates that move custody's funds, such as Swap on
Aquarius, proceeds to custody, don't use it; they go through the custody gate.

![Manage execution](/img/prime/manage-execution.webp)

## Portfolio

The **Portfolio** page shows balances for up to twenty EVM, Stellar or NEAR
addresses. The list is kept in your browser, and the app sends only the
addresses to our portfolio service to look up balances.

![The Portfolio page](/img/prime/portfolio.png)
