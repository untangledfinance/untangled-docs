# Recover custody funds

<video controls width="100%" preload="none" poster="/img/prime/video-07-recover-custody-funds.jpg" src="/video/prime/07-recover-custody-funds.mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.

If custody's signers lose access, the Prime Account's signers can move custody's
funds to the recovery address set in the gate, without custody signing. This
only works if the gate was created with a recovery address.

1. As a signer of the Prime Account, go to **Policies**. The custody gate card
   shows the recovery address.
2. Select **Recover custody funds**.
3. Choose the asset and the amount. The amount starts at everything the gate can
   move, minus the XLM reserve custody has to keep.
4. Select **Prepare in Prime Execution**. Prime Execution opens with one call
   that pays the amount from custody to the recovery address. It needs the
   Prime Account's normal approval count.
5. Select **Execute**, then **Run**, and sign.
6. Another signer opens the co-sign link from the Queue, checks the call and
   selects **Sign and continue**.

![The custody gate card on Policies](/img/prime/custody-gate-card.webp)

![The Recover custody funds dialog](/img/prime/recover-dialog.webp)

The recovery goes through the gate like any other move, so the gate's wait
applies. In the video the gate has no wait and the 250 XLM arrives straight
away.
