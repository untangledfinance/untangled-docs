# Create a Prime Account

<video controls width="100%" preload="none" poster="/img/prime/video-01-create-a-prime-account.jpg" src="/video/prime/01-create-a-prime-account.mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

You choose the signers and the approval count first. Nothing goes on chain until
you have reviewed them, and then one transaction creates the account with both
built in.

In the video, Signer B creates an account with three signers (Signer B, Signer A
and the Admin) and sets two approvals.

## Before you start

You need a Stellar wallet that can sign Soroban authorisations, such as
Freighter, with a little XLM for network fees. LOBSTR, xBull and Albedo cannot
approve Prime Account actions over their own connection; the app tells you if
your wallet is one of them.

## Connect and start

Open the app, select **Connect a wallet** in the top right and choose your
wallet. The wallet you connect becomes the account's first signer.

Open **Accounts** and select **Create a Prime Account**. The set-up has four
steps across the top: **Account**, **Add signers**, **Approvals** and **Review**.

## Account

The first step shows the address your Prime Account will have. The app works it
out from your wallet, so it is known before the account exists. Nothing is
created yet.

Do not send funds to this address before the account exists. XLM sent to an
address with no account at it is lost.

If you already have an account and want a second one, **Create another Prime
Account** starts again at a new address. Otherwise select **Next**.

![The Account step, showing the Prime Account address before it exists](/img/prime/onboard-account.webp)

## Add signers

Add every wallet that should be able to approve what the account does. Your
connected wallet is always one of them. Paste each address and select **Add
signer**. You can keep it to your own wallet and add others later.

![The Add signers step with two more signers added](/img/prime/onboard-add-signers.webp)

## Approvals

Choose how many signers must approve before the account does anything. With 2
of 3, any two signers together can act and no single signer can act alone.

![The Approvals step set to 2 of 3](/img/prime/onboard-approvals.webp)

## Review and create

Check the account address, every signer and the approval count. They are built
into the account when it is created. Changing them later is possible, and it
needs the approvals you set here.

Select **Create account**. Your wallet asks you to sign one transaction, and the
app shows it being built, simulated, signed, sent and confirmed.

![The Review step for a 2 of 3 account](/img/prime/onboard-review.webp)

## What you have now

**Accounts** lists your wallet with the new Prime Account under it.

![The Accounts page with the wallet and its Prime Account](/img/prime/accounts-page.webp)

The account can approve things, but it cannot use any funds yet. Next, custody
sets up the gate that lets it: [Set up the custody gate](./02.set-up-the-custody-gate.md).

To change the signers or the approval count later, see
[Signers and approvals](../04.reference/01.signers-and-approvals.md).
