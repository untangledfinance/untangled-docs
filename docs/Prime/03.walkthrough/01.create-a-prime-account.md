# Create a Prime Account

<video controls width="100%" preload="none" poster="/img/prime/video-01-create-a-prime-account.jpg" src="/video/prime/01-create-a-prime-account.mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

You need a Stellar wallet that can sign Soroban authorisations, such as
Freighter, and a little XLM for fees. LOBSTR, xBull and Albedo can't approve
Prime Account actions through their own extensions.

1. Select **Connect a wallet** in the top right and connect. This wallet will be
   the account's first signer.
2. Go to **Accounts** and select **Create a Prime Account**.
3. The first step shows the address the account will have. It doesn't exist yet,
   so don't send anything to it. Select **Next**. (If you already have an
   account, **Create another Prime Account** gives you a second address.)
4. Add the other signers by pasting each address and selecting **Add signer**.
   You can also stay the only signer and add people later.
5. Set how many signers must approve. The video uses 2 of 3.
6. Check the address, the signers and the approval count, then select **Create
   account** and sign.

![The Add signers step](/img/prime/onboard-add-signers.webp)

![The Review step for a 2 of 3 account](/img/prime/onboard-review.webp)

When the transaction confirms, **Accounts** shows your wallet with the Prime
Account under it.

![The Accounts page with the new Prime Account](/img/prime/accounts-page.webp)

The account has no access to custody's funds until custody sets up a gate for
it.

Next: [Set up the custody gate](./02.set-up-the-custody-gate.md).

You can change the signers or the approval count later; see
[Signers and approvals](../04.reference/01.signers-and-approvals.md).
