# Set up the custody gate

<video controls width="100%" preload="none" poster="/img/prime/video-02-set-up-the-custody-gate.jpg" src="/video/prime/02-set-up-the-custody-gate.mp4">
  Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

Custody does this step with its own wallet. The funds stay in the custody
wallet, and the gate gets an allowance to draw on them.

The set-up page is at
[app.untangled.finance/#/onboard/stellar/gate](https://app.untangled.finance/#/onboard/stellar/gate)
on mainnet, or the same path on beta.untangled.finance for testnet.
A Prime signer can also reach it from **Policies** (the **Set one up** link) or
from **Manage execution** on the Accounts page, and send the link to whoever
holds custody's keys.

## 1. Custody

Connect the custody wallet and open the page. The first step shows that wallet as
the account holding the funds. If it is a multisig, it also shows the weight
needed and the weight your key has. In that case each transaction below will
wait in the Queue for custody's other signers.

## 2. Destinations

Paste the address of the Prime Account the gate is for. The app fills in that
account's execution contract, the only caller the gate will accept.

Set the time lock:

- **Wait before a move runs** is the minimum number of ledgers every move waits.
  0 means the gate adds no wait and leaves it to each rule. A ledger is about
  five seconds.
- **Run window** is how long a move can still run after its wait. After that it
  lapses. 17,280 ledgers is about 24 hours.

These apply to every move through the gate, recoveries included.

![Wait before a move runs, and the run window](/img/prime/gate-time-lock.webp)

Add every venue the account will use, such as an Aquarius pool. On mainnet,
search for the pool by token or address; on testnet, paste its address. The
gate refuses any batch that involves an address not on its list. The Prime
Account, custody and the assets are added automatically.

The gate can pay any address on its list without further approval, so keep the
list short.

**Recovery address** is the wallet that gets custody's funds if custody loses
its keys. The app fills it in with a signer of the Prime Account. Change it to a
trustee's wallet if you have one, as in the video. Keep one: you can remove it,
but then nobody can move custody's funds once its signers are gone, and the app
warns you of that.

![Destinations and the recovery address](/img/prime/gate-destinations.webp)

## 3. Limit

For each asset, enter the amount the gate may release (or **Unlimited**) and the
number of days the allowance lasts, up to 180. **Add another asset** adds a
second one, such as USDC. When an allowance expires, nothing more can be drawn
in that asset until custody grants a new one.

![The Limit step](/img/prime/gate-limits.webp)

## 4. Review

Check the gate address, where it spends from, where it can send funds, the
limits and the time lock. Then select **Deploy gate and grant limits** and sign.

The destinations, time lock and recovery address can't be changed once the gate
is deployed. To change them you deploy a new gate. Allowances can be changed
later with a new grant.

![The Review step](/img/prime/gate-review.webp)

## Custody's other signers

The set-up sends one transaction for the gate and one per asset. If custody is a
multisig and its own key isn't enough, each one waits in the Queue. Each of
custody's other signers opens **Transactions**, then **Queue**, opens the item
and selects **Sign with active wallet**. When the weight is reached, the last
signer selects **Submit**.

![A gate deployment in the Queue at 10 of 20](/img/prime/queue-custody-weight.webp)

Once all of them are on chain, the set-up page shows the gate as live.

## Restrict custody's own keys

The gate limits what Prime can draw. It doesn't stop custody's own key from
moving custody's funds directly, outside Prime. To stop that, change the custody
account's signers so that no single key can move funds alone. On Stellar this
is a **Set Options** operation on the custody account.

Getting this wrong can lock custody's funds for good. Try the same change on
testnet first, and keep a recovery address on the gate.

Decide the new set-up before you build anything:

- the weight of custody's own key (the **master weight**);
- each other signer's public key and weight, from 1 to 255;
- the **low**, **medium** and **high** thresholds.

A transaction goes through when the weights of its signatures add up to the
threshold of its operations. Payments and contract calls, such as granting the
gate an allowance, use the medium threshold. Changing signers or thresholds uses
the high threshold. So the keys
you will still hold must reach the high threshold, or nobody can change the
account again. A master weight of 0 means custody's own key can't sign at all.

In the video, custody needs weight 20: its own key has 10, and Signer A and
Signer B have 5 each, so custody can't move funds without all three. If one of
those keys is lost, custody can no longer sign, and the gate's recovery address
is how its funds get out.

To make the change in [Stellar Lab](https://lab.stellar.org):

1. Choose the network (testnet first), then **Build Transaction** with the
   custody account as the source account, and select **Fetch next sequence**.
2. Add one **Set Options** operation for each signer, with its public key and
   weight. Each operation adds, updates or removes one signer, and a weight of 0
   removes it. Signers you don't mention stay as they are. An account can have
   up to 20 signers.
3. In one of the operations, set the master weight and the three thresholds.
4. Keep all the operations in this one transaction, so they apply together or
   not at all.
5. Sign with enough of custody's current keys to meet its current high
   threshold (for a plain account, its own key) and submit.

Then look the account up, in Stellar Lab or an explorer such as Stellar Expert,
and check every signer, weight and threshold. Back in Prime, the gate set-up
page's first step now shows the weight needed and the weight your key has.

From then on, custody's own transactions, such as granting a new allowance or
cancelling a stored move, wait in the Queue for its other signers. The gate's
draws within the allowance don't need custody's signatures.

The same applies on EVM, where the funds have to sit in a Safe; see
[Prime on EVM](../06.prime-on-evm.md#2-set-up-the-custody-gate).
