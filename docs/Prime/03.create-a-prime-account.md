# Create a Prime Account

This guide takes you from an empty screen to a working Prime Account with its
signers and approval threshold set. You give the signers and the approval count
first, and nothing goes on chain until you review it. You do not need to read any
code.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Stellar wallet that can sign Soroban transactions, such as Freighter.
- A small amount of XLM in that wallet to pay for the setup transactions.

## Connect your wallet

Select **Connect a wallet** in the top right, then choose your wallet. The wallet
you connect becomes the first signer of the account you are about to create.

![The connect-a-wallet dialog](/img/prime/connect-wallet.png)

## Start setup

Open **Accounts** and select **Create a Prime Account**. The wizard opens on an
**Account** screen and then walks three steps where you make the choices: Add
signers, Approvals, and Review. Nothing is deployed until you finish the review
step.

The **Account** screen shows the address your account will have. It is worked out
from your wallet, so it is known before the account exists. Do not send funds to
it yet: nothing is on chain there until you finish setup, and XLM sent to an
address with no account at it is lost. Select **Next** to carry on. If you
already run one account and want a second, **Create another Prime Account**
starts again at a new address.

## Step 1: Add signers

Add the wallets you want to be able to approve what the account does. Your
connected wallet is always one of them. Enter each address and select **Add
signer**. You can leave it as just yours and add others later.

![Step 1, adding two more signers](/img/prime/onboard-add-signers.png)

## Step 2: Set approvals

Choose how many of the signers must approve before the account does anything.
Two of three means any two of the three signers together can act, and no single
one can act alone.

![Step 2, setting a two-of-three threshold](/img/prime/onboard-approvals.png)

## Step 3: Review and create

Check the account address, the full signer list, and the approval count. The
signer set and approval count you choose here are fixed into the account when it
is created, so check them carefully before you go on.

When it is right, finish setup. Your wallet asks you to sign one transaction, and
one only. It creates the account with its signers and approval count built in, and
the account is ready the moment it confirms.

![Reviewing a two-of-three account](/img/prime/onboard-review.png)

## What you have now

Your Prime Account is live. It acts only when the approval count you set is met.
From here you can:

- See the account and grant it an allowance - see [Accounts and portfolio](./09.accounts-and-portfolio.md).
- Delegate a bounded key to an agent or service - see [Policies](./05.policies.md).
- Connect a venue and supply or swap - see [Apps and execution](./07.apps-and-execution.md).

To change the working signer set or approval count later, see
[Signers and approvals](./04.signers-and-approvals.md).
