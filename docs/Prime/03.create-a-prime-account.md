# Create a Prime Account

This guide takes you from an empty screen to a working Prime Account with its
signers and approval threshold set. You will sign two transactions with your
wallet. You do not need to read any code.

## Before you start

You need:

- A Stellar wallet that can sign Soroban transactions, such as Freighter.
- A small amount of XLM in that wallet to pay for the two setup transactions.

## Connect your wallet

Select **Connect a wallet** in the top right, then choose your wallet. The wallet
you connect becomes the first signer of the account you are about to create.

![The connect-a-wallet dialog](/img/prime/connect-wallet.png)

## Start setup

Open **Accounts** and select **Create a Prime Account**. Setup runs as four
steps: Activate, Add signers, Approvals, Review.

## Step 1: Activate the account

The first step shows the address your Prime Account will have before it exists -
the address is worked out from your wallet, so it is known in advance.

Select **Activate**. Your wallet asks you to sign one transaction and you approve
it. This deploys the account and makes your wallet its first signer. When it
confirms, the account exists on chain.

![Step 1, showing the account address before activation](/img/prime/onboard-activate.png)

Activation is safe to repeat. If you come back and activate again, it binds the
account that already exists instead of deploying a second one.

## Step 2: Add signers

Add the wallets you want to be able to approve what the account does. Your
connected wallet is always one of them. Enter each address and select **Add
signer**. You can also leave it as just yours and add others later.

![Step 2, adding two more signers](/img/prime/onboard-add-signers.png)

## Step 3: Set approvals

Choose how many of the signers must approve before the account does anything.
Two of three means any two of the three signers together can act, and no single
one can act alone.

![Step 3, setting a two-of-three threshold](/img/prime/onboard-approvals.png)

## Step 4: Review and finish

Check the account address, the full signer list, and the approval threshold. When
it is right, finish setup. Your wallet asks you to sign the second transaction,
which installs the signer set and the threshold.

![Step 4, reviewing a two-of-three account](/img/prime/onboard-review.png)

## What you have now

Your Prime Account is live. It acts only when the threshold you set is met. From
here you can:

- See the account and grant it an allowance - see [Accounts and portfolio](./08.accounts-and-portfolio.md).
- Delegate a bounded key to an agent or service - see [Policies](./05.policies.md).
- Connect a venue and supply or swap - see [Apps and execution](./07.apps-and-execution.md).

To change signers or the threshold later, see
[Signers and approvals](./04.signers-and-approvals.md).
