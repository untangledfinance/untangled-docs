# History

Each line has a **Stats** tab. The tab opens with the market of the strategy, then the **History** section, which shows the position's totals, its charts and its moves. Anyone can read the market figures. The History section needs a connected account.

![The Stats tab of tXAU · Aave V4 · Kamino with the market figures and the reserve charts](/img/prime/ly-kamino-stats-market.png)

## The market

| Section | Figures |
|---|---|
| **Market** | Kamino supply APY, Aave borrow APY, Carry, Kamino utilisation and Aave utilisation |
| **Liquidity** | Kamino supplied, Kamino borrowed, Aave available, Aave borrowed and the receipt token rate |

The reserve charts under these figures show the Kamino supply APY, the Aave borrow APY and both utilisation rates. Select **24h**, **7d** or **All** to change the range.

## The History section

The figures are read from Base and from the yield source.

| Line | Totals |
|---|---|
| Kamino | Borrowed, Repaid, Sent to Solana, In Kamino now, Earned on Kamino |
| OctoLend | Borrowed, Repaid, Sent to Stellar, In OctoLend now, Earned on OctoLend |

**Earned** is the value in the yield source now, plus what you withdrew from it, minus what you deposited.

Under the totals, the step charts show the borrowed USDC (**Borrowed**), the tXAU collateral (**Collateral**) and the value in the yield source (**In Kamino** or **In OctoLend**) over time.

> **SCREENSHOT NEEDED:** the History section of the Kamino Stats tab, with the totals, the step charts and the move table. Wide viewport (1920 x 1080). State: a connected wallet that owns a Prime Account with Kamino history.

## The moves

The table lists each move in the position, newest first.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Time | When the transaction was made, in UTC |
| Action | The move: **Supply tXAU**, **Withdraw tXAU**, **Borrow**, **Repay**, **Send to Solana**, **Back from Solana**, **Kamino deposit** or **Kamino withdraw**. OctoLend uses **Send to Stellar** and **Back from Stellar**. |
| Amount | The amount in USDC or tXAU |
| Transaction | A link to the transaction on its chain |

## While the history loads

The section shows a placeholder while the history is read. The first read shows the newest Solana or Stellar transactions, and the rest follow when they arrive. If the read fails, the section shows **Couldn't read your history**. Reload the page to read it again.

## Snapshot charts

Snapshot charts record the position and the reserves on a schedule, so the charts show how the rates and the position change over time. They sit in the same grid as the step charts.

Until they are enabled, the grid shows one **Enable** button.

- **Enable** asks your wallet to sign a sign-in message. It is not a transaction, and it moves no funds.
- Enabling installs the monitor templates for the line. On Kamino these cover the position, the Kamino reserve and the Aave V4 reserve. On OctoLend they cover the position.
- While the templates are set up, the section shows skeleton charts and the line **Setting up…**.
- A chart with no points yet shows a skeleton. A chart with one point shows a dot, and a chart with more points shows a line. The newest value shows in the chart header.
- An item the service cannot install shows as **Position: Not available now**, **Kamino: Not available now** or **Aave: Not available now**. The other charts still appear.
- If the set-up fails, the section shows **Setup failed** and a **Retry** button.

The service decides which fields each template returns. The panel labels them as **Supply APY**, **Borrow APY**, **Carry APY**, **LTV**, **Collateral tXAU**, **Debt USDC**, **Idle USDC** and similar names.
