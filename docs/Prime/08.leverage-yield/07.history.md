# History

Each line has a **Stats** tab with one section, **History**. It shows the position's totals and its transactions, read from the chains, and the snapshot charts.

## The figures

The figures are read from Base and from the yield source.

| Line | Totals |
|---|---|
| Kamino | Borrowed, Repaid, Sent to Solana, In Kamino now, Earned on Kamino |
| OctoLend | Borrowed, Repaid, Sent to Stellar, In OctoLend now, Earned on OctoLend |

**Earned** is the value in the yield source now, plus what you withdrew from it, minus what you deposited.

Under the totals, the step charts show the borrowed USDC, the tXAU collateral and the value in the yield source over time.

## The transactions

The table lists each move in the position, newest first.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Time | When the transaction was made, in UTC |
| Action | The move, for example **Borrow**, **Repay**, **Supply tXAU**, **Withdraw tXAU**, **Send to Solana**, **Back from Solana**, **Kamino deposit** or **Kamino withdraw**. OctoLend uses **Send to Stellar** and **Back from Stellar**. |
| Amount | The amount in USDC or tXAU |
| Transaction | A link to the transaction on its chain |

## While it loads

A chart area shows a placeholder while the history is read. The first read shows the newest Solana or Stellar transactions. The rest follow when they arrive. If the read fails, the section shows **Couldn't read your history**.

## Snapshot charts

The snapshot charts sit in the same grid, after the step charts, and the move list comes after them. Until they are on, the grid shows one **Enable** button in place of the charts. **Enable** turns on snapshot charts for this custody. The charts record a value on a schedule, so they show how the rates and the position change over time.

- **Enable** asks your wallet to sign a sign-in message. It is not a transaction and moves no funds.
- The charts appear after the service sets up the subscription. While it does, the section shows a placeholder and the line **Setting up**.
- A chart with no points yet shows a skeleton. A chart with one point shows a dot, and a chart with more shows a line. The newest value shows in the chart header.
- If the set-up fails, the section shows the reason in one line and a **Retry** button.
