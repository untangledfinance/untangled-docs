# Close a position

Close repays the whole USDC debt on Base and withdraws all the tXAU collateral to custody. The USDC comes back from the yield source: Kamino on Solana or OctoLend on Stellar, over CCTP.

To reduce the position without closing it, use [Repay and withdraw](./04.repay-and-withdraw.md).

## The Close tab

Select the **Close** tab on the **My position** card. The tab has no input. It shows three rows and one button.

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Owed | The USDC debt on Base |
| From Kamino, or From OctoLend | The USDC the Close brings back from the yield source |
| tXAU back | The tXAU collateral that returns to custody |

Select **Close**. The app reads the chain again, checks the amounts and opens the review.

:::caution
If custody does not hold the Circle USDC the repay needs, the card shows a shortfall panel above the button. The panel gives the amount short and custody's address, with a copy button. **Read again** reads the balances once more. **Add** asks your wallet to send the USDC custody lacks to custody, after its own review. After the Add, the Close can run.
:::

## The steps

| Line | Rows |
|---|---|
| Kamino, standing close rule | Withdraw from Kamino · Send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |
| Kamino, one signature | Withdraw and send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |
| Kamino, when the close rule is not standing | Allow · Withdraw from Kamino · Send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |
| OctoLend | Withdraw from OctoLend · Send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |

Each row shows its state. A finished row shows its time. The last row, **Repay and withdraw**, finishes the Close.

With signer B set up, a Close needs no wallet signature on the standing rule. Without signer B, the wallet asks for the Base transactions. See [Rules and signer B](./06.rules-and-signer-b.md).

## Deliver

Circle's forwarder sometimes does not mint the USDC on Base by itself. When that happens, the **Mint on Base** row shows **Deliver**, three minutes after Circle's attestation.

- **Deliver** asks your wallet to send Circle's message to Base, which mints the USDC to custody.
- With signer B set up, the browser delivers the message itself and the button does not appear. [NEEDS SOURCE: the delay before signer B delivers on the live app.]

## Repay and withdraw

If signer B does not show progress for three minutes while it repays, the card offers **Repay and withdraw** to your wallet. The button sends the repay from your wallet instead. The chain is read first, so nothing is repaid twice.

## Abandon

**Abandon** appears under the card only before any USDC has been burned. It asks for confirmation in a dialog titled **Abandon Close**. After the burn, the Close cannot be abandoned. Finish it with the steps above.

## How long it takes

Measured in test runs on 9 Nov 2026:

| Line | Time |
|---|---|
| Kamino, with signer B | 37 s and 24 s (two runs) |
| OctoLend, with signer B | 2 min 10 s (one run) |

When the mint needs **Deliver**, the run takes longer. In one run the Deliver button appeared 3 min 22 s after Circle's attestation.
