# Close a position

Close repays the whole USDC debt on Base and withdraws all the tXAU collateral to custody. The USDC comes back from the yield source: Kamino on Solana or OctoLend on Stellar, over CCTP.

To reduce the position without closing it, use [Repay and withdraw](./04.repay-and-withdraw.md).

## The Close tab

Select the **Close** tab on the **My position** card. The tab has no input. It shows three rows and one button.

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Owed | The USDC debt on Base |
| From Kamino, or From OctoLend | The USDC the Close brings back from the yield source |
| tXAU back | The tXAU collateral that returns to custody |

Select **Close**. The app reads the chain again, checks the amounts and opens the review. Select **Sign** in the review to start the Close.

:::caution
If custody does not hold the Circle USDC the repay needs, the card shows a shortfall panel above the button. The panel shows **Short by X USDC** and custody's address, with a copy button. **Read again** reads the balances once more. **Add** asks your wallet to send the USDC custody lacks to custody, after its own review. After the Add, the Close can run.
:::

## The steps

| Line | Rows |
|---|---|
| Kamino, standing close rule | Allow · Withdraw from Kamino · Send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |
| Kamino, when your wallet signs the Solana side | Withdraw and send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |
| OctoLend | Withdraw from OctoLend · Approve · Send to Base · Attestation · Mint on Base · Repay and withdraw |

Each row shows its state. A finished row shows the time it finished. The row in progress shows how long it has run. The last row, **Repay and withdraw**, finishes the Close.

**Allow** is done once the standing close rule is in place. The rule is installed during setup, or by the first Close. Your wallet signs the Solana side on its own only when the wallet can sign it and no rule has to be installed first. In that case the list shows **Withdraw and send to Base** in place of **Allow** and **Withdraw from Kamino**.

With the standing close rule in place, the Solana side makes no wallet prompt. With signer B set up, the Base repay makes no prompt either. Without signer B, the Base repay asks your wallet or custody's signers. See [Rules and signer B](./06.rules-and-signer-b.md).

## Deliver

Circle's forwarder sometimes does not mint the USDC on Base by itself. When Circle has attested the message and no mint arrives after a short wait, the **Mint on Base** row shows **Deliver**. The row also shows **Forward failed** when the forwarder reports a failure.

- **Deliver** asks your wallet to send Circle's message to Base, which mints the USDC to custody.
- Signer B waits for the mint before it repays. The row stays on the mint until the USDC arrives.

## Repay and withdraw from your wallet

If signer B makes no progress for a short wait while it repays, the card offers **Repay and withdraw** to your wallet. Signer B stops for this Close when you press it, so only one sender repays the debt.

If the mint does not arrive for a longer wait, signer B stops waiting and the card shows **Retry**. Select **Retry** to ask signer B again.

## Abandon

**Abandon** appears under the card only before any USDC has been burned. It asks for confirmation in a dialog titled **Abandon Close**. After the burn, the Close cannot be abandoned. Finish it with the steps above.

If another browser started a Close that this browser cannot see, the card shows **Close in progress** with its own **Abandon** button.

## Signatures

| Case | Wallet prompts |
|---|---|
| Standing close rule, signer B set up | None |
| Standing close rule, no signer B | The Base repay: one wallet signature when the Close role allows your wallet alone. Otherwise custody's signers approve the request in the **Queue**. |
| Your wallet signs the Solana side | One wallet signature for the withdraw and the send |
