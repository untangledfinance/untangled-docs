# Open a position

Open takes tXAU from your custody, supplies it to Aave V4 on Base, borrows USDC against it and sends the USDC to the yield source. You sign once in your wallet. The browser and the chains run the rest.

Setup must be finished first. See [Set up](./02.set-up.md).

## The form

With no position, the right-hand card is the Open form.

| Field | What to enter | Hint under the field |
|---|---|---|
| **Supply (tXAU)** | The tXAU to supply as collateral. The 25%, 50%, 75% and MAX buttons fill it from the tXAU in custody. | `In custody X tXAU` |
| **Borrow (USDC)** | The USDC to borrow. The 25%, 50%, 75% and MAX buttons fill it from what is available. | `Available X USDC` |

Leave **Supply** empty or 0 to send more of what custody already supplies. The transaction then has three steps instead of six.

The line under the fields shows the health factor after the Open. Below 1.6 the line reads **Health factor low**. The Open stops when the health factor after would be below 1.25. The form then shows **The health factor would fall to X, below the stop at 1.25.**

> **SCREENSHOT NEEDED:** the Open form with both fields empty. Wide viewport (1920 x 1080) and a phone width (390). State: a connected wallet, a finished setup, tXAU in custody and no position.

If the budget for the line is used up, the card shows **Budget used, refills** and the date of the next refill. If the market is closed, it shows **Market closed**. The Open button is off in both cases.

## Check the review

1. Select **Open**. The app reads the chain again and checks the amounts, the health factor and the budget.
2. The review shows the figures from that read:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Arrives at | The Solana account or Stellar account that receives the USDC |
| Arrives | The USDC that reaches the yield source, after Circle's fee |
| Fee | The most Circle's fee can take: up to 0.002 USDC on Kamino, and 0 USDC on OctoLend. See [Fees and gas sponsorship](./08.fees-and-gas.md#circles-fee). |
| Close reserve | The Circle USDC custody must hold so that a later Close can repay the debt. The row shows only when it is above zero. |
| Health factor after | The health factor once the Open is done |

3. Select **Sign** to send the transaction from your wallet, or cancel.

If custody does not hold the Close reserve, the form shows **Add X USDC to custody first** and an **Add** button. **Add** asks your wallet to send the amount custody lacks to custody, after a review of its own. Nothing else is sent.

## The steps

After you sign, the card shows the steps as rows. Each row shows its state. A finished row shows the time it finished and a link to its transaction where there is one. The row in progress shows how long it has run.

| Line | Rows |
|---|---|
| Kamino | Sign · Burn on Base · Attestation · Mint on Solana · Deposit to Kamino |
| OctoLend | Sign · Burn on Base · Attestation · Mint on Stellar · Deposit to OctoLend |

- **Sign** is your wallet transaction.
- **Burn on Base** sends the USDC to Circle's bridge.
- **Attestation** waits for Circle to sign the message. The row links to Circle's status for the burn. If Circle takes longer than usual, the row says so.
- **Mint** and **Deposit** run for the yield source. The browser key signs them on Kamino, and the keeper key signs them on OctoLend.

If a row fails, it shows its error line and the card offers **Retry**. Retry continues from the failed row. A burn that was already sent is not sent again.

> **SCREENSHOT NEEDED:** the run tracker while an Open is in flight, with the Attestation row active. This needs a signed Open. Capture it only with a signed run that Tuan has approved.

## After the Open

When the deposit is on the yield source, the finished run stays on the card. On Kamino the button reads **Done**, and on OctoLend it reads **View position**. Select it to go back to the form. The finished run leaves the card.

The position appears on the **My position** tab. See [Repay and withdraw](./04.repay-and-withdraw.md) and [Close](./05.close.md) for what you can do next.
