# Open a position

Open takes tXAU from your custody, supplies it to Aave V4 on Base, borrows USDC against it and sends the USDC to the yield source. You sign once in your wallet. The rest runs in the browser and on the chains.

Setup must be finished first. See [Set up](./02.set-up.md).

## The form

With no position, the right-hand card is the Open form.

| Field | What to enter | Hint under the field |
|---|---|---|
| **Supply (tXAU)** | The tXAU to supply as collateral. The 25%, 50%, 75% and MAX buttons fill it from the tXAU in custody. | In custody, with the amount held |
| **Borrow (USDC)** | The USDC to borrow. The buttons fill it from what is available. | Available, with the USDC the yield source can take |

The line under the fields shows the health factor after the Open. Below 1.6 the line becomes a warning, **Health factor low**. The Open stops when the health factor would fall below 1.25. The form then shows **The health factor would fall to X, below the stop at 1.25.**

If the budget for the line is used up, the card shows **Budget used, refills** and the date and time of the next refill. If the market is closed, it shows **Market closed**. The Open button is off in both cases.

## Check the review

1. Select **Open**. The app reads the chain again and checks the amounts, the health factor and the budget.
2. The review shows the figures from that read:

| Row | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Arrives | The USDC that reaches the yield source, after Circle's fee |
| Fee | The Circle fee, up to 0.002 USDC on Kamino. See [Fees and gas sponsorship](./08.fees-and-gas.md). |
| Close reserve | The Circle USDC custody must hold so that a later Close can repay the debt |
| Health factor after | The health factor once the Open is done |
| Solana account or Stellar account | The account that receives the USDC |

3. Select **Sign** to send the transaction from your wallet, or cancel.

If custody does not hold the Close reserve, the form shows **Add X USDC to custody first** and an **Add** button. **Add** asks your wallet to send the amount custody lacks to custody, after a review of its own. Nothing else is sent.

## The steps

After you sign, the card shows the steps as rows. Each row shows its state, and a finished row shows its time and its transaction.

| Line | Rows |
|---|---|
| Kamino | Sign · Burn on Base · Attestation · Mint on Solana · Deposit to Kamino |
| OctoLend | Sign · Burn on Base · Attestation · Mint on Stellar · Deposit to OctoLend |

- **Sign** is your wallet transaction.
- **Burn on Base** sends the USDC to Circle's bridge.
- **Attestation** waits for Circle to sign the message. The row links to Circle's status for the burn.
- **Mint** and **Deposit** run in the browser for the yield source. The browser key or the keeper key signs them.

If a row fails, it shows its error line and the card offers **Retry**. The page never sends a signed transaction twice.
