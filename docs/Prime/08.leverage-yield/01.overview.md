# Leverage Yield

Leverage Yield borrows USDC against tXAU on Aave V4 and sends the USDC to a yield source. The collateral and the debt sit in your custody on Base, under the rules of your Prime Account. The yield source is Kamino on Solana or OctoLend on Stellar. Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) moves the USDC between Base and the yield source's chain.

:::caution
Leverage Yield runs on the Base Sepolia test network only, with test funds. There is no EVM mainnet version yet.
:::

Open [beta-evm.untangled.finance](https://beta-evm.untangled.finance) and go to **Apps**, then **Leverage Yield**.

![The Leverage Yield list with its strategy cards](/img/prime/ly-strategy-list.png)

The list also shows **tXAU · Aave V4 set C**. The pages below cover the Kamino and OctoLend strategies only.

## The two strategies

| Strategy | Collateral and debt | Yield source | Where the USDC goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| tXAU · Aave V4 · Kamino | tXAU collateral and USDC debt on Base | Kamino lending market | Solana |
| tXAU · Aave V4 · OctoLend | tXAU collateral and USDC debt on Base | OctoLend lending market | Stellar |

Both strategies share one debt on Base. A custody can run either line or both.

## What you own

- **Collateral and debt:** the tXAU and the Aave V4 debt are held by your custody Safe on Base. The Prime Account can act on them only through the rules and limits in the custody gate.
- **The yield source position:** the Kamino position is held in your Solana account. The OctoLend position is held in your Stellar account.
- **Circle USDC in transit:** USDC is in transit only during an Open, a Close, or a Repay and withdraw run.

## What the keys do

| Key | Where it is held | What it signs |
|---|---|---|
| Your wallet | Your wallet extension | The setup steps your wallet signs, the Open, the custody steps you approve, and the Close steps your wallet signs |
| Browser key | This browser (Solana) | The Solana steps: deposit, redeem and the burn to Base, within the Solana rules |
| Keeper key | This browser (Stellar) | The OctoLend steps: deposit, withdraw, approve and burn, within the keeper rules |
| Signer B | This browser (Base) | Only the Base transactions the Close role and the repay role allow: the repayment and the delivery of a Circle message to custody |

Each key can do only what its rule allows. The rules and their limits are on the **Rules** tab. See [Rules and signer B](./06.rules-and-signer-b.md).
