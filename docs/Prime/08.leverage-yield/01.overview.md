# Leverage Yield

Untangled Prime lets institutions hold a real-world asset and augment its yield: borrow stablecoins against it through a policy-bound smart account and put them into higher-yield tokenised sources, with compliance, access control and privacy built in.

Leverage Yield applies that to one asset, tXAU, on Aave V4. You supply tXAU as collateral, borrow USDC against it on Base, and send the USDC to a yield source. Circle's Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol (CCTP) moves the USDC from Base to the yield source's chain.

## The two strategies

| Strategy | Collateral and debt | Yield source | Where the USDC goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| tXAU · Aave V4 · Kamino | tXAU collateral and USDC debt on Base | Kamino lending market | Solana |
| tXAU · Aave V4 · OctoLend | tXAU collateral and USDC debt on Base | OctoLend lending market | Stellar |

Both strategies share one debt on Base. A custody can run either line or both.

## What you own

- **Collateral and debt:** the tXAU and the Aave V4 debt are held by your custody Safe on Base. The Prime Account can act on them only through the rules and limits in the custody gate.
- **The yield source position:** the Kamino position is held in your Solana account. The OctoLend position is held in your Stellar account.
- **Circle USDC in transit:** USDC is in transit only during an Open, a Close or a repayment, and it arrives in your custody on Base.

## What the keys do

| Key | Where it is held | What it signs |
|---|---|---|
| Your wallet | Your wallet extension | The setup steps that your wallet signs, the Open, and the custody steps that you approve |
| Browser key | This browser (Solana) | The Solana steps: deposit, redeem and the burn to Base, within the Solana rules |
| Keeper key | This browser (Stellar) | The OctoLend steps: deposit, withdraw, approve and burn, within the keeper rules |
| Signer B | This browser (Base) | Only the Base transactions allowed by the Close role and the repay role: delivering a Circle message and repaying the debt |

Each key can do only what its rule allows. The rules and their limits are on the **Rules** tab. See [Rules and signer B](./06.rules-and-signer-b.md).
