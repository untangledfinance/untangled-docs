# Rules and signer B

Leverage Yield runs through roles on the custody gate, and through rules on the Solana and Stellar accounts. Each role or rule allows a fixed set of calls. This page describes what each one allows, and what signer B does.

## The Rules tab

The **Rules** tab lists the rules for each line, grouped by chain. Each card shows its name and what it allows.

| Section | Cards |
|---|---|
| Base | **Open once**, the Open role. **Auto Close**, signer B's Close role. |
| Solana | The Kamino deposit and redeem rule, the CCTP burn rule, and the close rule. [NEEDS SOURCE: the card names on the live app.] |
| Stellar | The keeper rules, one card for the four rules, with its expiry. [NEEDS SOURCE: the card name on the live app.] |
| Keys | Your wallet, the browser key or keeper key, and signer B |

Select a card to see its detail, and the trash icon to remove it. Removing a Base role needs custody's approval.

## The Close role

The Close role lets the custody gate, for the Prime Account, do one thing on Base: repay the whole USDC debt and withdraw all the tXAU collateral, to custody. In order, it:

1. approves the Aave V4 Spoke to take the Circle USDC for the repay,
2. repays the whole debt,
3. sets that approval back to 0,
4. withdraws all the collateral to custody.

The repay and withdraw amounts are fixed to the whole debt and the whole collateral. The role cannot repay part of the debt or withdraw part of the collateral. Its members are the Prime Account and signer B.

## The repay role

The repay role is installed once for each line. It is what **Repay and withdraw** uses. It lets the custody gate, for the Prime Account, do these calls on Base, in one batch:

1. approve the Aave V4 Spoke to take at most 6 USDC,
2. repay at most 6 USDC of the debt,
3. set that approval back to 0,
4. withdraw tXAU to custody, within the line's daily allowance.

| Limit | Value on this build |
|---|---|
| USDC repaid per run | 6 USDC at most |
| tXAU withdrawn per day | 0.1 tXAU per line, refilled every 24 hours |
| Health factor after a run | At least 1.5, checked in the app. The Aave V4 Spoke also refuses any withdraw that leaves the health factor below 1. |
| Withdraw recipient | Custody only |

[NEEDS SOURCE: the final values for the daily allowance and the USDC per run. The values above are this build's settings.]

The Kamino and OctoLend lines each have their own allowance. Withdrawing on one line does not use the other's.

## Signer B

Signer B is a key that lives in this browser. Custody adds it to the Close role, so the browser can deliver the Circle message and repay the debt without a wallet prompt.

### What signer B may sign

| Transaction | Condition |
|---|---|
| The Close call | Byte-for-byte the call the Close role defines: the whole repay and the whole withdraw |
| The repay call for a partial repayment | Byte-for-byte the batch the repay role defines, with the USDC and tXAU amounts of the run |
| Delivering a Circle message | A message that mints to your custody, from your own burn, at most 6 USDC |

Every call has value 0.

### What signer B may not do

- Sign any call that is not one of the calls above, even on the same contracts.
- Send tXAU or USDC anywhere other than custody.
- Change roles, members or allowances. Only custody can do that.
- Act on another custody's position.

### Signer B's ETH

Signer B pays the gas for its own transactions in ETH. When its balance falls below a small floor, the relayer can send it a top-up. The floor is 0.00002 ETH. [NEEDS SOURCE: the top-up amount and its limits on the live app.]

### Where signer B lives

- Signer B is made in this browser for one custody. The browser stores its key encrypted, in browser storage.
- **Forget** removes signer B from this browser. A browser that already holds a signer for the custody cannot make a second one.
- If you clear this browser's data, signer B is lost with it. Revoke the Close role first, and add a new signer B in another browser if you still need one.

### The signer B card

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Signer B is not in the Close role |
| **On** | Signer B is in the Close role, from this browser |
| **Needs ETH** | Signer B is set up, and its ETH is below the floor. **Funding** appears while the top-up runs. |
| **On in another browser** | The Close role has a signer B from another browser |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable** | Makes signer B in this browser. It has no address until then. |
| **Add to role** | Asks custody to add signer B to the Close role. Custody signs the change, once. |
| **Revoke** | Asks custody to remove signer B from the Close role |

Custody signs **Add to role** and **Revoke** through its own signers. If custody has more than one signer, the card shows **Waiting for co-sign** until the other signers approve in the **Queue**.
