# Rules and signer B

Leverage Yield runs through roles on the custody gate, and through rules on the Solana and Stellar accounts. Each role or rule allows a fixed set of calls. This page describes what each one allows, and what signer B does.

## The Rules tab

The **Rules** tab lists the rules for each line, in sections.

| Line | Sections | Cards |
|---|---|---|
| Kamino | **Base**, **Solana**, **Keys** | Base: **Open** and **Close**. Solana: **Kamino deposit and redeem**, **CCTP burn** and **Close**. Keys: the browser key and the Swig wallet. |
| OctoLend | **Setup**, **Base**, **Stellar**, **Keys** | Base: **Open** and **Close**. Stellar: **Keeper rules**, one card for the four rules, with its expiry. |

Select a card to see its detail. The remove control on a card takes the rule away. Removing a Base role needs custody's approval.

## The Close role

The Close role lets the custody gate, for the Prime Account, do one thing on Base: repay the whole USDC debt and withdraw all the tXAU collateral, to custody. In order, it:

1. approves the Aave V4 Spoke to take the Circle USDC for the repay,
2. repays the whole debt,
3. sets that approval back to 0,
4. withdraws all the collateral to custody.

The repay and withdraw amounts are fixed to the whole debt and the whole collateral. The role cannot repay part of the debt or withdraw part of the collateral. Its members are the Prime Account and signer B.

## The repay role

The repay role is installed once for each line. It is what **Repay and withdraw** uses. It lets the custody gate, for the Prime Account, do these calls on Base, in one batch:

1. approve the Aave V4 Spoke to take at most 6 USDC,
2. repay at most 6 USDC of the debt,
3. set that approval back to 0,
4. withdraw tXAU to custody, within the line's daily allowance.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| USDC repaid per run | 6 USDC at most |
| tXAU withdrawn per 24 hours | 0.1 tXAU per line |
| Health factor after a run | At least 1.5, checked in the app before the run is sent |
| Withdraw recipient | Custody only |

The Kamino and OctoLend lines each have their own allowance. Withdrawing on one line does not use the other's.

## Signer B

Signer B is a key that lives in this browser. Custody adds it to the Close role, so the browser can deliver the Circle message and repay the debt without a wallet prompt.

### What signer B may sign

For each call, signer B signs a typed message (EIP-712) that names that call. It does not send the transaction. The fee sponsor broadcasts the signed message on Base and pays the gas.

| Call | Condition |
|---|---|
| The Close call | The call the Close role defines: the whole repay and the whole withdraw |
| The repay for a partial run | The batch the repay role defines, with or without the withdraw, for the amounts of that run |
| Delivering a Circle message | A message that mints to your custody, from your own burn, at most 6 USDC |

Every call has value 0. Signer B signs only these calls, and it refuses to sign any other message it is given.

### What signer B may not do

- Sign any call that is not one of the calls above, even on the same contracts.
- Send tXAU or USDC anywhere other than custody.
- Change roles, members or allowances. Only custody can do that.
- Act on another custody's position.

### Gas for signer B

Signer B holds no ETH and pays no gas. The fee sponsor pays the gas for each message that signer B signs. Signer B has no balance to fund, so the card has no ETH states. [NEEDS SOURCE: what the card shows, and what you do, when the fee sponsor refuses a signed message.]

### Where signer B lives

- Signer B is made in this browser for one custody. The browser keeps its key in IndexedDB, encrypted.
- **Forget key** removes signer B from this browser. A browser that already holds a signer for the custody cannot make a second one.
- If this browser's data is cleared, signer B's key is lost. The Close role still lists signer B. Custody can take it out with **Turn off**.

### The signer B card

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Signer B is not in the Close role |
| **On** | Signer B is in the Close role, from this browser |
| **On in another browser** | The Close role has a signer B from another browser |

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enable** | Makes signer B in this browser, then opens a review to add it to the Close role. Custody signs the review. |
| **Add to role** | Opens the review to add the signer B this browser already holds to the Close role |
| **Use this browser** | Moves the Close role's signer B to this browser. Shown when another browser holds it. |
| **Turn off** | Opens a review to remove signer B from the Close role. Custody signs it. |
| **Forget key** | Removes signer B's key from this browser. Shown when this browser's signer B is not in the Close role. |

Custody signs the review for **Enable**, **Add to role**, **Use this browser** and **Turn off** through its own signers. While a change waits in the queue, the card shows **Waiting for co-sign**. The request also appears in the **Queue**, under **Waiting for other signers**.
