# Welcome to Prime

Prime is your own smart account on Stellar. Instead of a single private key that
can do anything, you get an account whose actions are checked against rules you
set before they run.

Prime is not a custodian and it is not where your funds sit. Your funds stay in
your own custody wallet; Prime is the authority over what can be done with them.

In practice:

- **A team can govern an account together.** An account can require more than one
  approval before it acts, so no one person can move funds alone.
- **You can delegate a key without trusting it.** Hand a key to an agent, a
  service, or a colleague, and bound what that key is allowed to do. If a call
  breaks the rule, the account refuses and nothing moves.
- **You keep the ability to change your mind.** You can add or remove signers,
  raise the number of approvals required, and revoke a delegated key at any
  time.

Prime runs on Stellar today. A cross-chain surface, PrimeX, is in development.

## What you can do

| You want to | Prime gives you |
|---|---|
| Require more than one approval before the account acts | A signer set and an approval threshold |
| Let an agent trade or claim yield for you, safely | A policy that caps what a delegated key can call and spend |
| Approve a transaction as a team | A co-signing flow that collects each signature in turn |
| Supply to a lending pool or swap on a venue | Ready-made policy templates for Blend and Aqua |
| Watch balances across your addresses | A portfolio view |
