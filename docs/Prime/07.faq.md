# FAQ

### What is a Prime Account?

A multisig smart account on Stellar, under rules you set: who can approve, how
many must agree, and what an agent may do on its own. It is not a custodian.
Your funds stay in your custody wallet, and the account may draw on them only
through the custody gate.

### Do my funds move into Prime?

No. They stay in your custody wallet, MPC or multisig. During a move, the gate
draws the amount from custody, the venue does its part, and the proceeds go back
to custody, all in one transaction.

### Do I need a new wallet?

No. Connect a Stellar wallet you already have, such as Freighter. It becomes the
first signer of your Prime Account.

### Can an agent take more than I allowed?

Not under its rule. The account refuses on chain any move outside the rule's
venue, amount band or approvals. One case to avoid: a key placed on more than one
rule is limited only by the loosest of them. Read
[What a policy does not protect](./05.security.md#what-a-policy-does-not-protect)
before you delegate.

### Can a move be stopped after the agent signs it?

Yes, if its rule has a minimum wait. The move is stored on chain, and until it
runs, custody or the account's signers can cancel it from the Queue. See
[Use the time lock](./03.walkthrough/06.use-the-time-lock.md).

### What if custody loses its keys?

If the gate was created with a recovery address, the Prime Account's signers can
move custody's funds there, and nowhere else, without custody signing. See
[Recover custody funds](./03.walkthrough/07.recover-custody-funds.md).

### What if I lose one of my Prime signer keys?

The approval count protects you. With 2 of 3, losing one key still leaves two
that can act, and they can remove the lost key and add a new one. Choose an
approval count where losing one key neither locks you out nor hands control to
whoever finds it.

### Can I change the signers or the approval count later?

Yes, on the Signers tab under Policies. A change takes two transactions, and the
account approves it like any other. See
[Signers and approvals](./04.reference/01.signers-and-approvals.md).

### What does it cost?

Stellar network fees, in XLM, for each transaction you sign. Creating the account
is one transaction. The gate is one transaction to deploy and one per asset
limit. Installing a rule, creating the execution contract and running a move are
one each, and a move under the time lock is two: one to store it and one to run
it. Keep a little XLM in each wallet that signs.

### Is Prime audited?

Not externally yet. The OctoGate contracts have had an internal adversarial
review and a threat model. Some supporting pieces are OpenZeppelin example
contracts we deployed ourselves and have not audited. See
[Security](./05.security.md).

### What if the app is unavailable?

The app is a convenience for reading and building transactions. It does not hold
your funds or your keys. The funds stay in your custody wallet, the Prime Account
is a contract on Stellar, the signers are your own wallets and the rules live on
chain. The interpreter's addresses and build hash are published, so the account
can be read and operated with other tools.

### Does Prime work on other chains?

It also runs on EVM, on the Base Sepolia test network only, where it works
differently. See [Prime on EVM](./06.prime-on-evm.md).
