# FAQ

### Where do my funds sit?

In your custody wallet. A swap sends its proceeds straight back there. A Blend
supply made through Prime is held in the Prime Account's name while it's open,
and withdrawing sends the funds back to custody.

### Do I need a new wallet?

No. Any Stellar wallet that can sign Soroban authorisations works, for example
Freighter. The wallet you connect first becomes the account's first signer.

### Can an agent go beyond its rule?

The account contract rejects anything outside the rule's venue, amount band or
approvals. Don't put the same key on more than one rule, though. See
[Security](./05.security.md#where-policies-fall-short).

### Can I stop a move after the agent has signed it?

Yes, if the rule has a wait. Custody or the account's signers can cancel it from
the Queue until it runs. See
[Use the time lock](./03.walkthrough/06.use-the-time-lock.md).

### What happens if custody loses its keys?

If the gate has a recovery address, the Prime Account's signers can move the
funds there without custody. See
[Recover custody funds](./03.walkthrough/07.recover-custody-funds.md).

### What if I lose a Prime signer key?

With 2 of 3, the other two signers can still act and replace the lost key. With
1 of N, one stolen key can act alone. With N of N, one lost key locks the
account.

### Can I change signers later?

Yes, from the Signers tab on the Policies page. It takes two transactions. See
[Signers and approvals](./04.reference/01.signers-and-approvals.md).

### What does it cost?

Stellar network fees in XLM. Creating the account is one transaction.
Setting up the gate is one plus one per asset, and the execution contract is two
(create it, then let it run stored moves). Installing a rule or running a move
is one each, and a time-locked move is two (store, then run).

### Is it audited?

An external audit is in progress. See [Security](./05.security.md#audit-status).

### What if the app goes down?

The funds are in your custody wallet, and the account and its rules are
contracts on Stellar, so they keep working without the app. Pending co-sign
requests are held by our service until they're submitted. The interpreter's
addresses and build hash are published, so you can check what the account runs
without relying on our app.

### Is there an EVM version?

Yes, on Base Sepolia only. It works differently; see
[Prime on EVM](./06.prime-on-evm.md).
