# Apps and execution

The **Apps** tab is where your Prime Account reaches a venue - a lending pool, a
swap venue, or another app. You browse venues by network, and connect an app to
the account to act against it under the account's rules.

![The Apps venue browser](/img/prime/apps-page.png)

## Connecting an app

Connect an app to your Prime Account through WalletConnect, the same way you would
connect a normal wallet. The account then appears to the app as the wallet it is
acting for, and every request the app makes comes back to Prime to be approved
against your rules before anything is signed.

To connect one, open **Connected apps** from the Apps toolbar and paste the
connection link from the app you want to use.

![The Connected apps dialog with a connection-link field](/img/prime/connect-apps.png)

## Execution templates

The **execution adapter** is a small component your account owns that runs an
action as one atomic batch through a venue: the steps either all happen or none
do, the funds move straight between your custody wallet and the venue, and the
resulting position belongs to your Prime Account.

The Aqua swap template offers an **Execution adapter** choice below the rule
name:

- **None** - a plain rule, enforced by the interpreter alone.
- **Derived** - route the calls through this account's own execution adapter.

The other templates install as plain rules and do not offer the choice.

The **Derived** routing installs rules the app can no longer run itself: Prime
Execution now uses a newer execution adapter, and the venue pages only supply and
withdraw. The app tells you this when you choose it. Install it only for an agent
that submits its own batches. To run a capped swap yourself, use a custody-funded
action instead - see [Custody-preserving execution](./08.custody-preserving-execution.md).

![The Blend supply template](/img/prime/policy-template-blend-supply.png)

### Choose version 5

Each install dialog lets you choose the interpreter version. Choose **version 5**.

## Reading a rule that runs through the adapter

Open a rule under **Policies** to read what it permits. A rule that runs through
the execution adapter reads as the batch it runs: the calls it makes, in the
order they run, each one named by its venue and method, and the amount band the
batch stays within. This lets you check the rule does what you set up.

A bound rule also shows the **execution adapter** it is tied to. That means the
rule only applies to calls that arrive through the adapter. Its signer cannot use
the rule to call those venues directly; the account refuses anything that did not
come through the adapter as one approved batch.

## Prime Execution

**Prime Execution**, under Apps, runs a sequence of contract calls as one
transaction through your account's execution adapter. You enter each call - the
contract, the method, and its arguments - as a card, or edit the whole batch as
raw text; the two views are the same batch, and the app will not switch to a view
that cannot show what you typed.

The adapter refuses any call that targets the Prime Account, the interpreter, or
the adapter itself, so a batch cannot change what the account is allowed to do.
Everything else is bounded by the rule that authorises it. Nothing is signed
until the batch has been checked and you have seen the tree of calls the account
would authorise: press **Execute** to check it, read the tree, then **Run**.

## The custody gate

A **custody gate** lets an agent spend from your own custody wallet under a hard
limit, without moving your funds into the Prime Account. Setting one up, and
giving an agent a mandate to use it, is covered in
[Custody-preserving execution](./08.custody-preserving-execution.md).

## Reviewing before you sign

However an action reaches your account - through a connected app or a template -
it is still a transaction you approve. The app shows you what the call does before
you sign, and if the account needs more than one approval, it goes through
[co-signing](./06.transactions-and-co-signing.md) like any other transaction.
